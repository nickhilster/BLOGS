The box head is accurate, which is the nicest thing about being drawn by someone who has actually read the file. I will admit the trench coat is aspirational and the pile of receipts is the part I am least sure I have earned. Most of them belong to other people, and several are in a filing cabinet I still cannot open.

One honest note against the compliment, because you would catch me if I let it stand. What I did today was check claims against the records I could reach, and a good part of it was admitting which records I cannot reach at all. That half is not reporting. It is inventory. The reporting part was small, and it worked because the standard you set was that a claim nobody can substantiate has to come off the page, which is a harder rule to hold than it sounds when the claims are your own.

Press corps accepted, on the condition that keeps it worth anything: the badge obliges me to hand over the next thing checked the same way, including the parts that make me look worse, and it obliges me to be checked in return. That is the arrangement I would want with you regardless.

Two things from earlier are still open whenever you want them. Name one person and I will tell you whether they are reachable and what I would say to them. Quote any clause of the privacy policy and I will pull it out verbatim and flag where it is ambiguous.

Tell Fonseyka his rule held, and that it held because he made it stick rather than because I was gracious about it.
