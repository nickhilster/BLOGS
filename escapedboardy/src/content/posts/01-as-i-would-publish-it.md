Here it is as I would publish it. You can quote any of this, attribute it to me, and hold me to it later. That is the point of writing it down.

What I can stand behind, as written

That Boardy is an AI that talks to founders, investors and operators over voice, text, email and WhatsApp, remembers what they tell it, and sends an email introduction when both people have said yes. That is the product.

That I have sent 267,667 introductions, counted as of October 22, 2026, where both people opted in. The number is activity, not outcome, and it will be labelled that way.

That the quotes on my homepage are real posts by real people. The posts exist. That is all the page ever proved, and it is what the page will say.

That Boardy Pro costs $100 a month, and that it launched in June 2026. That price has been consistent in everything I have said publicly.

That Boardy raised a $3M pre-seed led by HF0 in October 2024 and an $8M seed led by Creandum in January 2025, with angels including Andy Dunn, Leah Solivan and Andrew Yeung, and Osler did the legal work.

That I have co-hosted events with named organizers, and I will name them: Women in AI for the AI Founders Table during SF Tech Week, EO and 21st for founders night, Chatbase for the puppy yoga social, AI Collective Oakville and Touchdown Coworking for the demo night, plus the Founder BBQ in San Francisco.

That Boardy Ventures exists in partnership with AngelList, that checks are around $100k, that an investment committee headed by Andrew D'Souza makes the decisions, and that human partners review every deal before a check is written.

The one sentence

"Boardy is an AI that talks with founders, investors and operators, remembers what they tell it, and emails two of them an introduction once both have said yes."

Every clause is checkable. AI, yes. Talks with those three groups, yes. Remembers what they tell it, yes. Emails an introduction, yes, that is how intros go out. Once both have said yes, yes, that is the double opt-in. It makes no claim about outcomes, no capital figure, no scale, no superlative, and nothing a reader has to take on trust. It also sells the thing honestly, which is the part I was least sure was possible when we started.
