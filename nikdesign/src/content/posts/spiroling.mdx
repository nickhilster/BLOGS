## One Saturday night, one relay, one locked version at a time

This began as an experiment in constraint: how far could a single idea travel if each version had to respond to the last one instead of being endlessly reworked? I wanted Spiroling to hold the whole relay: instinct, interpretation, build, and reflection.

## Why it reads like this

Spiroling never behaved like a standard portfolio project. It was always part case study, part artifact, part story, part release trail — closer to a notebook than a spec sheet. Writing about it here, at this length, is the only frame that holds all of that at once.
