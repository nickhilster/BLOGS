Tonight a scam text slipped through my phone's spam filter — a fake Canada Post parcel fee. I asked Muse why it hadn't warned me. What followed wasn't really about the text. It was about the exact boundary between what an agent can do and what only an operating system can do — and why that boundary is the reason Kin OS exists.

The exchange, verbatim (my three thinking-out-loud status lines omitted):
