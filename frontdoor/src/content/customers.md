# Documented Paradox customer list — Operation Front Door

**Compiled:** Friday, November 6, 2026 (America/Toronto), by Muse. **Second pass added the same day** (see §5): 21 further companies from Paradox's press-release boilerplate (BusinessWire), Paradox's newsroom and blog, vendor-produced success-story PDFs and the vendor's FeaturedCustomers reference profile, Workday customer stories, and trade coverage (InsideSAP, Israel21c). The separate addendum file is preserved alongside this list; §5 below is the merged form.
**Method:** Names were taken only from fetched public pages: Paradox's own client-stories index and case-study pages (paradox.ai), Paradox's Client Board attendee page (clientboard.paradox.ai), a Workday press release (newsroom.workday.com, 2026-01-08), KrebsOnSecurity's July 2025 Paradox reporting, an independent S&P 100 ATS audit (airecruiter.co, checked Nov 2, 2026), and secondary industry compilations where marked. Every row carries its source URL.

**Honest caveat — read first:** Paradox publishes **no single exhaustive, verified customer list**. This is a *documented-evidence compilation*, not a census: a company appears here only where a fetched source names it, and absence from this list proves nothing. Evidence strength varies by class and is labelled per row: a vendor case study is the vendor's own claim; a Client Board listing shows an organisation's employees registered for Paradox's client event (Apr 2026 session), which Paradox presents as a client gathering but is attendance evidence, not a deployment record; independently reported rows rest on third-party reporting of varying depth, flagged in the notes. Deployments also change over time — Muse's own live checks on 2026-11-06 found **no** assistant at two vendor-documented customers (Medtronic, Tractor Supply web), which is recorded in their rows rather than smoothed away.
